INTERNAL BRIEF
Website Redesign Plan — ubcbim.com
A short overview of why we are redesigning our website and what the new site needs to deliver.
For internal review and discussion.
1. Why We Are Doing This
Our website holds strong content — our services, client testimonials, the machines and software
we support, and our track record across all the countries we serve and hundreds of projects.
The issue is presentation. The current site looks like a traditional brochure rather than a
technology company, and it gives visitors only one way to reach us: a single contact form.
Competitors in our space are setting a higher bar. As a reference point, NexGen Steel runs an
interactive 3D Virtual Lab where a visitor can pick a project, rotate the 3D model in their
browser, read its specifications and the machines used, and request a quote — all on one page.
It immediately signals a serious, modern engineering business. We offer a broader range of
services; we need to present them with the same level of polish.
Our goal
Keep our proven content, redesign it into a modern technology brand, add an
interactive 3D project showcase and sample files, and make it effortless for clients to
reach us — with every enquiry captured automatically in our CRM.
What the new site must achieve
1. Look and feel like a technology company, not a contractor brochure.
2. Prove our capability visually — an interactive 3D model showcase plus downloadable
sample files.
3. Give clients several easy ways to reach us: live chat, online call booking, and quote
buttons throughout.
4. Capture every lead automatically into our CRM, and use social media to bring in more
visitors.

3. The Key Priorities
3.1 An interactive 3D Project Lab (our centerpiece)
This is the feature that will most change how the site feels. We can export finished Revit models
to a web 3D viewer (for example, Sketchfab, which has a free Revit plug-in) and embed them on
our pages, so visitors can rotate and zoom with no software to install.
Each project should show:
• The 3D model (rotate, zoom, fullscreen).
• A specs panel: size, units, location, building type.
• Capability tags: the machine(s) and software used, and what we delivered (model, BOM,
CSV, permit set).
• Two actions: “Download sample files” and “Request a similar quote.”
• Filters by Residential / Commercial / Multifamily / Light-Gauge Steel / Wood.
Recommended start: 8–12 of our best wood and light-gauge steel projects for launch, then
grow the library over time.
3.2 Easy ways for clients to reach us
• Live AI chat widget on every page, logged into our CRM.
• “Book a 15-minute call” scheduler so clients pick a time directly — helpful across time
zones.
• WhatsApp / quick-call button for messaging-first clients.
• A sticky “Request a Quote” button that stays visible while scrolling, plus quote buttons
inside the 3D Lab.
3.3 Capturing every lead in our CRM
Every contact form, quote request, chat, and file download should land in our CRM
automatically — assigned to the necessary team members and tagged with where it came from.
The simplest reliable method is embedding web-to-lead forms built inside the CRM(we already
have it on out current site), with auto-assignment and lead-source tagging turned on. Live chat
and online booking feed the same records, so nothing is missed.
Note: We have already connected CRM with our current website, and have recently connected
our Meta account with the CRM. We have to further connect it with LinkedIn and any other
lead-generating forms or ways we do it - including the live AI chatbot that we will be integrating
into our website.
3.4 Sample files and capability information
Clients evaluate a partner by looking at real deliverables and checking compatibility. A
“Resources” hub should offer downloadable, watermarked samples — a sample 3D model, a Bill
of Materials, machine CSV files, and a permit set — behind a short form that creates a tagged

lead. A clear capability matrix (machines and software we support, and the file outputs clients
receive) answers their main questions at a glance.
3.5 Social media that brings in clients
• Prominent social icons in the header and footer, with our latest LinkedIn and YouTube
content embedded near the footer.
• Project walkthrough videos embedded on the 3D Lab and service pages; short model
clips posted to social media linking back to the site.
• Trackable links so we can see in our CRM which platform brings each lead and focus
effort where it pays off. LinkedIn is our priority channel for B2B clients.
3.6 A modern look and feel
• One deliberate design system — consistent colors, fonts and spacing across every
page.
• Real 3D models and renders instead of stock photos; subtle, purposeful motion rather
than carousels.
• Fast and mobile-first, with SEO basics (clear headings, page descriptions, image alt text)
on every page.
