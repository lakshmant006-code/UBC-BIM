Clickable annotation on the model — how a visitor drills into wall, truss or MEP detail.

```jsx
<Hotspot x="28%" y="46%" label="Wall panel · 2×6" onClick={() => open('wall')} />
<Hotspot x="72%" y="30%" label="Roof truss" leader="left" active />
```

Keep to three or four per view; more and the drawing stops being readable.
